# LinkedIn evidence map: show the method and keep the original outcome traceable

My public portfolio makes an engineering approach inspectable: define the decision, build a narrow implementation, test the failure modes, measure the result, and state what would change the next decision. A new reference implementation can demonstrate that approach. It cannot recreate a historical employer or client outcome.

The 19 rows below map the current LinkedIn experience bullets: eight CSAA, six independent startup advisory, and five independent advocacy. The statements in the second column are profile claims supplied for the rewrite, not figures verified by this repository. The last column names the original evidence needed to substantiate them. Private records should remain private unless publication is authorized; an approved, redacted summary or a reference can support a conversation without exposing customer data.

The portfolio now spans nine current implementations: the six-project collection plus three separate repositories, [Hybrid Fraud GNN](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/hybrid-fraud-gnn), [Dispatch ETA Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/dispatch-eta-lab) and [Tree Search Ranker](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/tree-search-ranker). Link the relevant repository directly when adding work samples. The [repository directory](repository-directory.md) also lists the five earlier projects restored separately on the selected account.

## Where the other public work fits

[Grocery Batch Planner](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/grocery-batch-planner) and [Fulfillment Policy Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/fulfillment-policy-lab) demonstrate independent fulfillment methods: actual duration learning and constrained assignment, checked fallback plans, logged-policy support, cross-fitted reward modeling, and frozen heldout evaluation. Their synthetic results do not validate a resume's historical grocery trial, incentive lift, customer deployment or assignment-service latency. Link them as new independent projects or related public implementations.

[Agent Replay Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/agent-replay-lab) adds local workflow recovery with leases, fencing, conservative synthetic attempt accounting and outbox deduplication. [Decision Memory Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/decision-memory-lab) adds source-bound revision records and bounded handoff context. They complement advisory architecture discussions, without demonstrating Claude/LangGraph deployments, production uptime, customer adoption, provider spend or historical workshops.

[Temporal Recommendations](../projects/temporal-recommendations/) complements these 19 bullets by showing a trainable PyTorch ranking experiment, temporal data boundaries, full-catalog evaluation, coverage accounting and a restrained release decision. Its MovieLens result is relevant method evidence for the separate ACE recommendation work. It does not prove Gaana's online CTR or dwell-time figures, Paytm transaction scale, Rapido pricing latency, advertiser onboarding improvements, or any other ACE outcome.

[Dispatch ETA Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/dispatch-eta-lab) broadens the independent engineering evidence with actual CPU XGBoost regression, past-only uncertainty calibration and explicit promise-policy accounting. Its fictional disruption shows why lower point error and narrower intervals can still produce worse decision loss. It supports a discussion of uncertainty, drift and operational acceptance criteria; it does not establish a real dispatch deployment, customer savings or historical pricing latency.

[Hybrid Fraud GNN](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/hybrid-fraud-gnn) and [Tree Search Ranker](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/tree-search-ranker) provide focused model-training artifacts alongside the existing risk and retrieval components. The first retains the stronger engineered graph baseline; the second requires independent judgments before deployment because its relevance rules are authored. Their numerical results and limitations remain in their own repositories rather than being presented as previous CSAA or advisory outcomes.

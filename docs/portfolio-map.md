# The portfolio: thirty-five current implementations and five earlier starting points

**Start with [six selected work samples by experience](selected-work-by-experience.md)** for recommendations/fulfillment, fraud/review signals, and independent inference/training systems. This is a curated reading route through the larger portfolio, with each new experiment identified as independent work.

**Here is how I frame, implement, evaluate and explain a system.** I start with a decision narrow enough to test, make the usual failure mode visible, build the boundary that addresses it, and keep the observed result beside its denominator and limitation. The next action follows from that evidence, including when the result favors caution.

The thirty-five implemented projects have their own code, data contracts and recorded runs. Twenty-six are publicly available; nine remain prepared locally for a later selected batch. The [November 9 directory](new-projects-2026-11-09.md) groups the 22 new case studies, records publication state and maps them to role-specific discussions. The thirteen earlier public implementations are detailed below: seven standalone repositories and six bundled projects. The [repository directory](repository-directory.md) also links the five earlier projects restored as separate repositories, alongside their preserved archives. This progression makes my methods inspectable without presenting new experiments as historical employer or customer deployments.

## Choose the engineering question

| Project | Narrow question and implemented framework | Recorded outcome and practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Grocery Batch Planner](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/grocery-batch-planner) | Can predicted shopping times support a feasible assignment? Actual CPU XGBoost, past-only buffer calibration, OR-Tools CP-SAT, independent verifier and bounded-search fallback. | Across **600** synthetic orders in **25** held-out waves, buffered CP-SAT assigns **284** on time versus greedy **275**, both with zero realized late assignments. Unbuffered CP-SAT yields **302** on time and **13** late: buffering reduces lateness while also reducing volume. The simulator is a single-wave abstraction, not a grocery service or field trial. |
| [Fulfillment Policy Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/fulfillment-policy-lab) | Do randomized logs support a proposed offer policy? Overlap/ESS gates, group cross-fitted reward model, validation-only selection, frozen heldout IPS/DR and cluster uncertainty. | On **2,400** synthetic test offers in **60** zone-days, selected-policy DR utility is **0.6554**, with a **[0.6129, 0.6967]** cluster interval. Utility is arbitrary units, not completion points or dollars. The validation-selected policy is not simulator-best; unsupported premium offers are refused. No real marketplace causal effect is measured. |
| [Agent Replay Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/agent-replay-lab) | Can a DAG resume without duplicating committed local effects? SQLite leases/fencing, pre-attempt synthetic reservations, atomic result/outbox commit and receiver deduplication. | Four tasks finish with **four unique local receiver effects and zero duplicates in each of five** deterministic scenarios. Restarts orderly close/reopen connections within one Python process; this is not killed-process, power-loss, provider-billing or production-reliability validation. |
| [Decision Memory Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/decision-memory-lab) | Can required current evidence survive a smaller handoff? Tenant-scoped revision resolution, source-byte checks, expiry/conflict refusal and atomic required-fact reservation within serialized UTF-8 limits. | Meets **14/14** authored contracts versus **10/14** for validity-aware recent-first packing and **1/14** for an intentionally incomplete recent-first baseline. The output contains untrusted data; no downstream LLM quality, prompt-injection resistance or token/cost saving is inferred. |
| [Hybrid Fraud GNN](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/hybrid-fraud-gnn) — standalone | Does a learned graph encoder add value beyond useful tree controls? Actual CPU XGBoost, a trainable two-layer inductive mean-SAGE encoder, prior-seven-day graph snapshots, disjoint synthetic cohorts and fixed review capacity. | On **1,600** synthetic test decisions with **126** positives and **80** reviews, engineered graph statistics find **19** positives, the trained hybrid **10**, and static trees **7**. Their AP values are **0.1725/0.1164/0.1133**, respectively. The trained hybrid's AP difference from static has a descriptive interval **[-0.0090, 0.0166]**. The learned stage has not earned its complexity in this run; carry the simpler graph-statistics candidate into representative evaluation. No production fraud, GPU or employer-metric claim is made. |
| [Dispatch ETA Lab](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/dispatch-eta-lab) — standalone | Does lower ETA error produce a better delivery promise under shift? Actual CPU XGBoost versus OLS, known-at-dispatch features, past-only split-conformal calibration, disjoint route/courier cohorts and explicit late/slack decision loss. | Across **1,800** fictional test deliveries, XGBoost/linear MAE is **7.20/7.50 minutes**. XGBoost interval coverage falls from **812/900 (90.22%)** before the declared disruption to **80/900 (8.89%)** afterward. Under the fixed illustrative weights, upper-endpoint promise loss favors linear **14.01** over XGBoost **15.98**. This is a synthetic uncertainty and decision audit, not a real dispatch service, causal ROI or guaranteed conformal coverage under shift. |
| [Tree Search Ranker](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/tree-search-ranker) — standalone | Can a tree combine retrieval evidence, authority and current/historical intent while preserving access boundaries? Scoped BM25 and TF-IDF/SVD, a common candidate union, 17 observable features, grouped XGBRanker and query-family splits. | On **72** test queries from **three** held-out fictional families, XGBRanker NDCG@5 is **0.9699**, versus **0.7706** for validation-selected BM25/SVD fusion and **0.8110** for BM25. The tree-minus-fusion family-bootstrap interval is **[0.1377, 0.2578]**. The permitted path produces zero forbidden candidates across the fixture. Authored relevance rules and a known static catalog limit generalization; this is document ranking, not LLM answer quality or production authentication. |
| [Evidence Gate](../projects/evidence-gate/) | Is a relevant passage admissible for this requester? BM25 retrieval, tenant/role authorization, validity/approval/scope gates, conflict handling, citations and answer/clarify/abstain contracts. | The gated system makes **24/24** correct task decisions on a constructed holdout, versus **5/24** for the relevance-only ablation under the same authorization boundary. It answers 17 cases and handles the other seven through clarify/abstain. This is fictional extractive retrieval, not measured generative-model accuracy or customer generalization. [Metrics](../projects/evidence-gate/results/metrics.json). |
| [Approval Workflow Lab](../projects/approval-workflow-lab/) | What exactly did the reviewer approve? Trusted principal boundary, tenant-isolated read-only SQL, registered KPIs, distinct requester/reviewer, signed transaction claims, version checks and atomic mutation/receipt/audit. | **40/40** evaluation contracts and **8/8** development cases; 29 evaluation cases require rejection. Six committed mutations match six execution audit events. An eight-thread replay applies one change and returns seven cached receipts. These authored protocol cases do not measure an LLM planner or prove a production identity/security service. [Metrics](../projects/approval-workflow-lab/results/metrics.json). |
| [Point-in-time Risk](../projects/point-in-time-risk/) | Does a review model use only what existed at decision time, and can its feature state recover? Synthetic events, delayed labels, temporal windows, atomic SQLite replay/checkpoints, late-event quarantine and chronological comparison. | On **3,045** test events with **78** positive synthetic outcomes, temporal boosting has AP **0.1356** versus static logistic **0.0835**. At the same 142-review daily-budget aggregate, it finds 23 versus 21 positives. The model-class comparison is a limitation; a separate static-boosting diagnostic is reported. This is offline synthetic ranking, not production fraud capture or distributed feature-store scale. [Metrics](../projects/point-in-time-risk/artifacts/metrics.json). |
| [Graph Risk Investigations](../projects/graph-risk-investigations/) | Does prior shared-device context improve a fixed review queue? Time-respecting graph features, entity-disjoint cohorts, identical fixed boosted-tree parameters, capacity accounting and label-free analyst cards. | On **2,700** synthetic test events with **77** positives, graph/static AP is **0.1096/0.0429**; positive reviews are **18/126 versus 9/126**. The gain shrinks from +8 positives before the declared mechanism shift to +1 after it. This supports a representative offline pilot, not deployment or a production fraud claim. It is graph feature engineering, not a GNN. [Metrics](../projects/graph-risk-investigations/reports/metrics.json). |
| [Temporal Recommendations](../projects/temporal-recommendations/) | Does personalization earn its complexity after the time and candidate boundaries are fixed? PyTorch BPR versus popularity, global temporal split, validation-only selection, full eligible catalog, coverage and paired uncertainty. | On **51** eligible MovieLens test users, BPR/popularity NDCG@10 is **0.1636/0.1377**. The paired difference interval **[-0.0196, 0.0664]** includes zero, and the warm-only policy covers **10.26%** of positive test ratings. A separately labeled fallback diagnostic reaches a broader population; it is not a confirmatory model lift. Keep popularity while collecting stronger evidence. No online CTR or dwell-time effect is measured. [Metrics](../projects/temporal-recommendations/reports/metrics.json). |
| [Inference Systems Lab](../projects/inference-systems-lab/) | Can a serving measurement survive malformed streams, rejected answers, fallback and missing usage? Bounded SSE transport, typed quality/SLO gates, per-attempt accounting, configurable vLLM/Nebius paths and an actual small PyTorch attention/KV experiment. | The committed HTTP fixture completes **12** known-answer loopback requests. The CPU attention run verifies cached/full outputs within tolerance; seven-trial medians are approximately **4.11/12.06 ms** for cached/full recomputation on the tiny untrained layer. This is neither LLM quality nor a serving speedup. A separate [free Tesla T4 CUDA check](../projects/inference-systems-lab/results/colab-t4/) passed its float16 correctness gate, with full/cached seven-trial medians of **25.626/20.790 ms**. This tiny layer is not a serving benchmark or a CPU/GPU comparison. vLLM serving, Nebius and Nsight remain unexecuted. [Metrics](../projects/inference-systems-lab/results/metrics.json). |

The quantities above belong to different experiments. Combining their case counts into a single accuracy score would mix protocol contracts, synthetic model evaluation, offline recommendation ratings and a known-answer transport fixture.

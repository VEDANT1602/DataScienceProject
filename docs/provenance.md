# Original work, references and dataset terms

This portfolio was developed for Vedant Thaker with AI implementation assistance. The problem framing and report structure draw on Vedant's supplied examples of decision-first analysis, evidence audits, explicit limitations and executive communication. The source prompts remain private.

The implementations are new portfolio work. No past employment dates, customer adoption, business savings or workplace performance results are inferred from these experiments.

## Conceptual starting points

[Build Your Own X](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) is a curated index of tutorials, rather than a single project or dataset. Its AI model, neural network, search engine, database and distributed systems categories motivated the selection of problems here.

Evidence Gate relates to the index's [RAG from Scratch](https://github.com/langchain-ai/rag-from-scratch) reference. Risk explores the availability and replay questions behind a database-backed ML workflow. Recommendations explores the index's neural-network and search/ranking themes.

Tutorial code, tutorial benchmark numbers and third-party result narratives were not copied into these projects. References provide context; repository experiments provide the reported results.

## Data boundaries

- **Evidence Gate:** original fictional documents and evaluation cases. No employer or customer records.
- **Point-in-time Risk:** deterministic synthetic transactions. The generator defines the task; results cannot establish real fraud effectiveness.
- **Temporal Recommendations:** MovieLens obtained from [GroupLens](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/100k/). The raw dataset is excluded from this repository. Follow the project's data card and official terms before downloading or using it.
- **Graph Risk Investigations:** original synthetic events and opaque entity identifiers, with strict prior-context features and disjoint split entities. Synthetic fraud labels are experiment ground truth, not allegations about real people.
- **Approval Workflow Lab:** fictional SaaS tenants and authored protocol cases. The deterministic planner is not a tested LLM, and trusted principals simulate an upstream authentication boundary.
- **Inference Systems Lab:** original local streaming fixtures, five fictional contracts and tiny seeded PyTorch tensors. Actual local CPU measurements remain separate from the subsequent free Colab Tesla T4 CUDA arithmetic run. vLLM serving, Nebius and Nsight paths remain unexecuted. Optional Colab/model use follows the linked provider/model terms.
